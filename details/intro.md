Predictions use models, hence quite a bit of this orientation touches on modelling. It does so, somewhat obscurely, in [Stage 3](#modelling) under "[Building trust in model](#identifying-models)". The reason is that in this context the modelling steps primarily serve to justify the choice of the model(s). How to model, and which model to use when is discussed under "[Bones of contention](#bones)".

A model is a flexible thing here: it can be a set of expert statements, a process model, or a deep-learning analysis. A prediction is a quantitative statement of a model, including forecasts and projections.

As a teaser, here are a few points to ponder:

A "context" explanation points to a system defined too narrowly, and may lead eventually to incompressible system descriptions if everything always remains context-dependent.

Keep the long-term view of science in mind.

The quality of predictions is currently unknown due to lack of uncertainty quantification.
